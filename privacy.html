Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Channel Studio is a private tool used only by its developer to manage YouTube channels that the developer owns. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Channel Studio uses the YouTube API Services to access, for that channel only: channel information, video information, analytics and revenue reports, and permission to upload and update the channel's videos and thumbnails.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner their own statistics and to publish their own videos. It is not used for advertising and is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and downloaded reports are stored only on the developer's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local copies of reports can be deleted by the developer at any time.

Google and YouTube policies

Channel Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the issues page of this site's GitHub repository.
